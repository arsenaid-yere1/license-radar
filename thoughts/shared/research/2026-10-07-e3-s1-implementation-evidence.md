## Implemented behavior and boundaries

`src/app/practice/calendar/page.tsx:PracticeCalendar` and `src/app/practice/register/[credentialId]/page.tsx:CredentialDetail` derive practice authority from current authenticated access and reuse `getMaintenanceRegister(..., false)`. Every active role can read. No-practice users go to onboarding. Unavailable reads remain safe errors; malformed, missing, foreign, or archived details use neutral not-found. Native event/detail/back/refresh links make document requests for fresh authority and data.

`src/lib/calendar/events.ts:projectCalendar` filters active records, emits one event for each entered end/action date, preserves cycle identity and date revision, marks the existing tracking date, and keeps both-unknown records in a separate list across months. Coverage matches direct ownership or covered clinician without multiplying events. Similar credentials stay distinct. `dates.ts` supports exact months in years 0001–9999 and practice-local today; `query.ts` bounds and validates scalar URL state, preserves encoding/context, and fails closed on repeated or invalid filters.

`CalendarPanel`, `MonthCalendar`, and `CalendarAgenda` share that projection. Desktop defaults to month; phone defaults to agenda, with explicit overrides. At phone width, month weeks reflow into wider labeled day cards; all events wrap. Shared `RecordDates` preserves existing date and unknown labels in register/detail. Calendar navigation is inside the existing register maintenance lock. SMS remains visibly inactive. No schema, RPC, grant, generated-type, reminder job, dashboard, successor cycle, or new editor is added.
